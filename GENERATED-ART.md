# Japanese visual assets

Created using the built-in image_gen tool. These are conceptual illustrations, not documentary images of medical facilities.

- `dist/assets/japan-washi.png`: Warm ivory handmade washi fibers, pale sage sumi-e mountains along the bottom, fine gold seigaiha waves lower right, muted vermilion sun upper right. Quiet negative space for dark green typography. No words or logos.
- `dist/assets/care-journey.png`: Editorial paper-cut and watercolor illustration in ivory, forest green, sage, gold and vermilion. A continuous mizuhiki cord connects consultation, a laboratory and a Japanese garden. Abstract figures; no actual hospital likeness, medical claims, text or logos. Captioned as an AI illustration on the website.
